WHO WE BUILD FOR
The advisors are the ones who are going to use this tool. We want to make it as simple as possible for them to use.
advisors are likely not extremely technical. We want to make it as simple as possible for them to use. 
their day to day workflow is likely very manual and they may not be excited for a tool that takes away the "crunch"

WHAT WE CUT
Fuzzy search - absolutely a great feature but tuning fuzzy search is a time consuming task and given that advisors have 
personal connections to their assigned students a simple string match will be sufficient until otherwise noted.

HOW WE USED AI
AI was used for scaffolding the entire project including writing the base docker files, and client server boilerplate.
During that time I needed to install some build packages and  AI pointed me to the correct packages but was mistaken
on the syntax of my package manager so I had to combine the two.

AI suggested commonjs modules in tsconfig which I noticed as an outdated pattern. I had to correct that and let
the AI know we were on NodeNext
